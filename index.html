Positional Stability Analysis of Quasar 4C31.61 Using VLBI Time Series and Allan Standard Deviation

Journal of Physics Conference Series | arXiv 2401.12325

Quasar 4C31.61 (2201+315) is an essential celestial reference point in the International Celestial Reference Frame (ICRF). To evaluate its positional stability, we analyze multi-year VLBI position time series using the Allan standard deviation method.

Allan Variance Formula

$$ \sigma_y^2(\tau) = \frac{1}{2(N-1)} \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} (\bar{y}_{i+1} - \bar{y}_i)^2 $$

This model helps separate white frequency noise processes from random walk positional changes caused by plasma jets or structural variations at high resolution.
